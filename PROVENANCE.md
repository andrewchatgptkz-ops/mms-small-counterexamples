## Independent verification (all by separately written code)
- 4×11: literal 4^11 enumeration (Python and C++), envelope certificate (114 surviving 4-partitions, no 2+2 hand-out), reproduced on the author's machine.
- 5×13: literal 5^13 (7 s, C++), subset DP, packing certificate 99/90/1746/1520/0.
- 6×15: subset DP, packing certificate 145/147/38473/42520/8066/7967/0 (9796 at q = 27), literal 6^15 = 470 184 984 576 allocations in 36 shards (about 25 min on 2 cores; visited count exactly 6^15).
- 7×17: two subset DPs (C++ and Python/Numba by Pro; C++ by Claude), packing certificate 230/247/174099/1117997/34965/29833/0 (102508 at q = 26), weight certificate by brute force over 2^17 subsets. No literal 7^17 enumeration (2.3·10^14).
- 8×19: subset DP by Claude (C++, max-min 103, MMS 104), packing certificate 326/324/…/0 at q = 104 and 56 tuple pairs (5 distinct-mask pairs) at q = 103, all four leaf certificates by brute force over 2^19 subsets in separately written code; the parametric formula checked at ε = 1/8, 1/6, 1/5, 1/4, 3/10, 1/3, 2/5 by packing counts on the scaled integer instances; the 6×15 locality counterexample checked by packing count.
- All weight certificates, chain certificates and the depth-2 certificate: brute force over all 2^m subsets (`verify/verify_certificate.py`, seconds).
- 2026-09-02 and 2026-09-05, on the author's own machine (Claude Code, Opus 5, separate code from the Cowork sessions): every instance re-checked end to end — grid structure (rows and columns really partition the goods and sum to T, no good worth T or more), subset DP (MMS = T for both types, max-min = T − 1), the packing counts recomputed by a different algorithm (union masks of the disjoint tuples plus a subset-sum transform instead of pairing tuples), and every weight certificate re-checked with the weights parsed from the table in `certificates/weight_certificates.md` rather than from the script. The 6×15 case was additionally re-run by literal enumeration with a separately written sharded program (about 26 minutes on two cores): exactly 470 184 984 576 = 6^15 allocations visited, none with minimum ≥ 28, best minimum 27. Added 2026-09-05: the 4×12 (T = 60) certificate; the 8×19 (T = 104) instance — subset DP (MMS = 104, max-min = 103), packing certificate (326 minimal R-sets, 324 C-sets, 0 compatible pairs at q = 104; 56 at q = 103), all four leaves of the depth-2 certificate by brute force over 2^19 subsets, and the decomposition 4·(score-2 profile) + (h_R, h_C) recomputed entry by entry; the rigidity correction (at n = 5 column c4 weighs 24, E = b − 1 and D + z = 22 − a on all three chain instances) and the locality counterexample (the perturbed 6×15 has max-min 28 = T) reproduced independently.

## Literature checked
2026-08-26 (manual): Google Scholar "cited by" of arXiv:2104.04977 sorted by date, 20 newest citing titles; homepages of Feige, Sapir, Tauber, Hummel; Zenodo web index; GitHub repository search; Wikipedia "Maximin share". 2026-09-02 (targeted, tool-assisted): no upper bound below 39/40 posted since 2021 (39/40 restated as current through August 2026); the fractions 29/30, 28/29, 27/28, 26/27 do not occur in the MMS literature; no prism/subdivision family found. The two-type row/column grid with equal row and column sums is Feige–Sapir–Tauber's own general construction (their Theorem 4 family); what is new here is the sparse prism realisation, the much smaller instances, the ratios, f(4) = 10 and the certificates. Not checked systematically: citation lists on Google Scholar / Semantic Scholar / OpenAlex after 2026-08-26, and Garg–Shahkar's citing papers.
